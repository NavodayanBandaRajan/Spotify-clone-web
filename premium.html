Frequently Asked Questions

What is Spotify Premium?

Spotify Premium lets you enjoy ad-free music, offline downloads, unlimited skips, and high-quality audio.

How do I cancel Premium?

You can cancel your Premium subscription anytime from your account settings. Your Premium benefits will continue until the end of your current billing period.

Can I download songs with premium?

Yes. Premium members can download songs, albums, and playlists to listen offline without an internet connection.

Can I share my Premium plan?

Yes. Duo and Family plans allow multiple people to enjoy Premium on separate accounts, according to the plan's eligibility rules.

Is there a free trial?

Spotify may offer a free trial for eligible new users. Availability depends on your country and current promotions.
